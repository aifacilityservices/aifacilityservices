About AI Facility Services

Our Mission

At AI Facility Services, our mission is to provide exceptional cleaning, maintenance, and repair solutions that exceed client expectations. We believe that a clean, well-maintained property reflects positively on your business and enhances your quality of life.

Why Choose Us

Professional Expertise: Our team is trained, experienced, and certified in all aspects of facility cleaning and maintenance. We stay current with industry best practices and use the latest equipment and techniques.

Reliability: When we commit to a project, we deliver. Our track record speaks for itself with satisfied customers across Toronto and the GTA.

Transparency: We believe in upfront pricing with no hidden fees. What you see is what you get, and our estimates are accurate and competitive.

Customer Focus: Every client is unique, and we tailor our services to meet your specific needs. We work with you to create a customized solution that works for your budget and schedule.

Quality Guarantee: We stand behind every project. If you're not satisfied, we'll make it right. Your satisfaction is our top priority.

Our Services

From residential cleaning to large-scale commercial projects, we offer a comprehensive range of services including:

  • Commercial and residential cleaning
  • Dryer vent and ductwork cleaning
  • Commercial kitchen deep cleaning
  • Parking lot line painting and striping
  • Facility maintenance and repairs
  • Concrete and surface restoration
  • Junk removal and disposal
  • Snow removal (seasonal)

Serving the Greater Toronto Area

Based in Toronto, we proudly serve the entire Greater Toronto Area and surrounding communities. Whether you need a one-time deep clean or ongoing maintenance services, we're here to help.
